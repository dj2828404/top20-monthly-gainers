    How this list is built and what the trigger labels mean

    Prices come from the NSE daily bhavcopy files (EQ and BE series). Return is measured from the last close of the previous month to the latest close of the month shown, adjusted for splits and bonuses. Stocks listed during the month, SME-platform stocks and BSE-only stocks are not included. Market cap is from Screener.in and is used only for the minimum market cap cut-off. There is no liquidity filter, so thinly traded names can appear.

    For each stock, a script collects exchange announcements, quarterly numbers and news headlines, and an AI model (Claude) writes the explanation from that evidence. "No clear trigger" means nothing public explains the move. A trigger is the most likely explanation, not proof of cause.
